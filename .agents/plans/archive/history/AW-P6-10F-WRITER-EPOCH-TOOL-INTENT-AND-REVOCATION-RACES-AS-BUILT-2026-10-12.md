Tool/Process execution requires additional original Agent/StepGate checks; the verified defect is precisely **durable new-effect-intent admission** on a stale writer, not demonstrated arbitrary execution of an OS Process. This could confuse effect custody and no-replay provenance.

The original assistant had also proposed another possible Tool Write. **Before fixing source, the new assertion FAILED:** the epoch-1 `SessionHandle` could append a NEW `ToolStarted` under epoch 3.
