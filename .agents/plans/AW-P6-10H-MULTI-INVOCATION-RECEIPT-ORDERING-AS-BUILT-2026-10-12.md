## 2. Native negative-first findings, original Store policy boundary

**Bug A: accepted overlapping and skipped provider ordinals.** Original `crates/aw-store/src/journal.rs::SessionHandle::append_sync` validates provider actor/identity, `(1..=32)` ordinal, SQLite WriterEpoch, current Session and exact `InvocationStarted` evidence. However it lacked a lower-level requirement that the next original paid invocation have **exactly the next sequential ordinal AND no prior unresolved `started` or `unknown` invocation**. A current trusted Store client could start ordinal 2 without ordinal 1, start ordinal 2 while ordinal 1 was still in flight, or skip ordinal 2 after closing ordinal 1. This violates the one-original-Agent serial provider execution and paid attempt audit model, even though the original hosted Agent itself serializes provider calls. Test `aw_p6_10h_original_provider_invocation_ordinals_are_sequential_and_serial` was authored BEFORE code change and **FAILED on its first negative assertion**: an initial paid `InvocationStarted(ordinal=2)` was accepted by the lower SQLite Store.

**Bug B: malformed original provider terminal outcome could launder billing certainty.** Previous AW-P6-10G distinguishes known provider `response_received + Some(CompletionMeta)` from trusted `not_dispatched + None` and unknown `failed_or_cancelled/stream_rejected + None`. But the lower `InvocationFinished` accepted impossible pairs (`response_received` without CompletionMeta; `not_dispatched` with metadata; `failed_or_cancelled` with metadata) and arbitrary terminal outcome strings; some could be written as factually `received`, suppressing unknown vendor charge evidence. New `aw_p6_10h_original_provider_terminal_metadata_must_match_outcome` was authored BEFORE code change and **FAILED on the first malformed pair**. The lower Store remains a trusted-host boundary, not cryptographic provider response attestation; it must still reject inconsistent factual evidence before committing durable receipts.
